# S02 offline indexability evidence

Experimental local analysis extracted from SEOsmith PR34 commit `26e1c68ef318386b3c79dedd4378c7d606977ff2`. It consumes only caller-supplied decoded HTML, individual retained HTTP field values, and retained robots decisions. It performs no fetching, collection scheduling, persistence, delivery, or live audit. The caller is responsible for source authenticity, URL binding, completeness, and supplying robots evidence appropriate to the page and bot.

`None` is unavailable, not an empty observation; incomplete or omitted HTML/HTTP evidence cannot establish permission. A known restriction remains a restriction under partial evidence. Robots denial does not reveal page directives: blocked content remains unknown. The robots matcher retains matched line, raw line and rule when reconstructable; disagreement or missing provenance makes the effective crawl decision unknown. Decisions are evaluated separately for each named bot, including search and training bots. Applicable source occurrences and their locators are retained for inspection; conflicts are observations, not automatic defects.

Intent is an explicit caller-supplied policy input, never inferred from crawl evidence. An unspecified intent or unknown index decision yields an unknown comparison. These local types are not a storage schema, delivery contract, provider indexing observation, complete robots implementation guarantee, or evidence that S02 is complete. Expiry is not evaluated against a clock; applicable expiry makes indexability unknown unless a definite restriction applies. Comma-containing header dates may make other bots unknown because their continuation is ambiguous. Header parsing is conservative around ambiguous syntax; supplied completeness flags and budgets cannot establish that an upstream collection was actually complete. Synthetic fixtures do not represent live sites.
